## Policy for filling the gaps
- Obtain usable, permission-cleared photographs from the venue's press/media kit or open-licensed Wikimedia Commons assets.
- Verify that the image depicts the named venue or specific experience, not merely a similar spa, nightclub, dining room, or vehicle.
- Track attribution and licenses here. Do not present editorial art as real venue photography.
- For CC0 pictures, give source where available. For CC BY/SA, comply with attribution, license link and downstream share-alike obligations.
